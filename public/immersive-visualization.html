Example concept: explore a spatial model from connected viewpoints.

From complex models to a shared understanding.

Immersive visualization turns plans, models, and technical information into a space people can explore. Instead of interpreting a drawing in isolation, viewers can move between viewpoints, inspect details, and discuss the same design in context.

  1. Prepare the scene. Bring together approved 3D assets, reference material, and the key questions the experience should answer.
  2. Explore and explain. Move through the environment, switch relevant layers, and use annotations to explain important details.
  3. Review together. Use the experience in a presentation, on a desktop, or in a supported immersive setup to collect focused feedback.

Illustrative use case

An infrastructure design review

Imagine a team reviewing a proposed facility. A guided walkthrough shows access routes, major spaces, and equipment locations. Reviewers can compare viewpoints and isolate a system layer to understand how it fits into the wider design.

The aim: make design intent easier to communicate and capture questions while changes can still be discussed.
